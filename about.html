About Natalia Sharova

Natalia Sharova is a Brooklyn-based abstract painter drawn to the beauty of things we feel but cannot name.

For over a decade, she has used lyrical abstraction to explore the realms of thought, feeling, and memory. Using palette knives and dry brushes, she builds soft fields of color and disturbs each layer with intuitive gestures, fine lines, and rich textures. The result is work that feels dreamlike and alive, where softness meets spontaneity, and every mark holds a trace of movement.

Growing up in Moscow, Natalia watched her mother paint moody oil landscapes and still lifes on canvases she stretched by hand. Her mother taught her not only how to draw, but how to see beyond the surface and break complex forms down into essential shapes and tonal relationships.

Her life and work have since taken her across the United States, from Wyoming to Washington DC and now Brooklyn, the place she calls home and where she maintains her studio residency.
