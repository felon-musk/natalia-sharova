Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Each painting is carefully boxed by hand in the studio and shipped fully insured, with tracking. You’ll receive a tracking number by email as soon as it’s on its way.

We currently ship within the US only. If you’re outside the US and interested in a painting, please get in touch and we’ll do our best to arrange delivery.

Sales

All sales are final. Each painting is one of a kind; please feel free to ask questions or request more photos before purchasing.

If your painting arrives damaged, email us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the painting and packaging, and we’ll make it right.

Payment

Checkout is handled securely by Stripe. Your payment details are never stored on this site.
